# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- Customize the Northern Vengeance reflecting shield independently of the Staff of Protection shield.
- Adjust color, glow, light brightness, opacity, and ovality for either shield. Both appearance profiles are enabled by default.
- When both mods are installed, an enabled Improved Magic Shield profile takes priority for that shield. Turning it off lets the enabled EffectsControl profile take over.
- The F1 menu shows a notice only for shields currently controlled by Improved Magic Shield.

## 1.0.1

- Local visual settings apply to other players' weapon effects and magic shields.

## 1.0.0

- Weapon light brightness, glow, opacity, size and particle count controls.
- Independent general-setting influence for attack launch, projectile flight and impact.
- Magic shield light brightness, glow, opacity, ovality and color controls.
- One whole-number hue slider for shield color.
- Whole-number settings and immediate updates through F1 Configuration Manager.
- Original visuals restored when the mod or its effect controls are disabled.
